# Statistics Projects

## 1. Descriptive Statistics: The Clinic Wait Time Outlier
- **Objective:** Analyze patient wait times to determine the "normal" patient experience.
- **Dataset:** `[10, 12, 11, 13, 14, 150]` (in minutes).
- **Skills Used:** Mean (Average), Median, identifying Outliers.
- **Key Finding:** The Mean wait time is 35 minutes, but the Median is 12.5 minutes. The single 150-minute outlier heavily skews the Mean. For a healthcare setting, the Median is a more reliable metric for patient experience.
