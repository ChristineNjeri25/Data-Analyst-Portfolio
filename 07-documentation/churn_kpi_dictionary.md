# KPI Dictionary: Customer Churn Rate

## 1. KPI Name
Customer Churn Rate

## 2. Business Definition
The percentage of customers who cancel their subscription or stop doing business with the company during a specific time period (usually monthly or quarterly).

## 3. Calculation Formula
(Number of Customers Lost During Period / Total Number of Customers at Start of Period) * 100

## 4. Data Source
CRM System (e.g., Salesforce), Subscription Database, or Billing System.

## 5. Target/Threshold
- **Good:** < 5% annually (B2B SaaS standard).
- **Warning:** 5% - 10%.
- **Critical:** > 10% (Requires immediate executive intervention).

## 6. Business Context & Root Cause Analysis
A high churn rate is not always a product issue. Analysts must check for:
- **Geographic anomalies:** Is churn localized? (e.g., Germany only).
- **Pricing shocks:** Did a promotional discount expire? 
- **External factors:** Competitor pricing, inflation, or macroeconomic downturns.
