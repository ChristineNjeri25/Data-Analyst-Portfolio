# Finance & Accounting Projects

## 1. Financial Statement Analysis & CoA Mapping
- **Objective:** Understand the three core financial statements (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) and how to map raw transactions to a Chart of Accounts (CoA).
- **Skills Used:** Gross Profit & Net Income calculation, CoA classification, and analyzing the difference between Profit and Cash Flow.
- **Key Finding:** A company can show a net profit on paper but still go bankrupt if their cash flow statement shows negative operating cash (e.g., unpaid invoices). Analysts must look at both statements, not just the P&L.
- **Business Logic:** Cutting marketing expenses to zero increases short-term Net Income, but poses a severe long-term risk to revenue generation and pipeline health.
