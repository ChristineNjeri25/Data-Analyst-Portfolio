# Power BI Projects

## 1. Sales Commission Dashboard
- **Objective:** Visualize total and average commission payouts by region to identify top-performing regions and individual earnings.
- **Skills Used:** Data ingestion (CSV), Interactive Visualizations (Clustered Bar Chart), DAX Measures (`SUM`, `AVERAGE`), and Cross-filtering.
- **Key Finding:** While the North region has the highest *total* payout ($5,600), the South region has the highest *average* payout per person ($3,000).
